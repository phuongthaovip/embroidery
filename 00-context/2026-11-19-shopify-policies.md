## Refund policy

All products at EmbroThread are **digital embroidery files**. Because digital files cannot be returned once they are delivered, **we do not offer refunds or exchanges** after your files have been sent.

Before you buy, please check:
- You have an embroidery machine and software that reads one of the listed formats.
- The design size fits your hoop.

**Problems with your files**
If a file does not open, is missing, or does not stitch correctly, email business@embrothread.com with your order number and machine format. We will fix or resend the file. If we cannot solve the problem, we will issue a refund.

**Orders not yet delivered**
If you change your mind before your files are sent, contact us and we will cancel and refund your order.

## Shipping policy

EmbroThread sells **digital files only**. Nothing is shipped by mail and there are no shipping fees.

Your embroidery files are sent to the email address on your order within **1–3 business days** after purchase. Please check your spam or promotions folder if you do not see them.

Did not receive your files after 3 business days? Email business@embrothread.com with your order number.
