## 3. Prompt

STYLE dùng chung:
```
Style: realistic machine embroidery mockup with satin and fill stitch texture, flat colors only (max 4 thread colors), bold clean lettering at least 0.25 inch tall, no gradients, no shading, no fur texture, no tiny details or thin lines, simple shapes easy to digitize. IMPORTANT: no outer border, no patch edge, no sticker outline. Fully transparent background (PNG with alpha channel), no white background, no fabric, no hoop, no shadow.
```

A cute Nordic folklore lineup embroidery design: five small characters standing side by side in a row: a black Yule cat with yellow eyes, a straw Yule goat with red ribbons, a cute Krampus with curved horns, a Mari Lwyd white horse skull wearing a red ribbon and bells, and a tiny Tomte gnome with a red hat. Arched above in bold red letters: "Yule Folklore Club". Spell exactly. Cute and friendly, same size characters. Black, red, cream, golden yellow. + STYLE`

Cute storybook style. Black, red, cream, green. + STYLE`
