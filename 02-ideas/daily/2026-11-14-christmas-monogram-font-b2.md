Completely flat white #FFFFFF background, no checkerboard.

**Ảnh 1:** "Create a square 1:1 image. The words "Merry Christmas" written in [mô tả font]. Thread colors: [...]." + Style

> Now create a square 1:1 image of the complete uppercase alphabet in exactly the same font, decorations and thread colors as the previous image. All 26 letters exactly once each, in 4 centered rows: row 1 "A B C D E F G", row 2 "H I J K L M N", row 3 "O P Q R S T U", row 4 "V W X Y Z". Every letter the same size and style. Plain solid pure white fabric, flat white #FFFFFF background, no frame, no title, no checkerboard.
