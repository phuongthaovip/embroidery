## 4. Prompt chung (thay [CHỦ ĐỀ] và [DANH SÁCH])
```
Create a square 1:1 image. A set of 12 separate small kawaii Christmas [CHỦ ĐỀ] machine embroidery designs
arranged in a neat 4x3 grid, each design isolated with space around it, every item has a cute happy face
(two small black dot eyes, tiny smile, pink oval blush cheeks), thick dark brown outlines, cozy palette of
red, forest green, cream, white, gold and soft pink. Items: [DANH SÁCH 1)–12)].
Style: realistic machine embroidery mockup with satin and fill stitch texture, flat colors only
(max 6 thread colors per design), no gradients, no shading, no tiny details or thin lines, simple shapes easy
to digitize. IMPORTANT: no outer border, no patch edge, no sticker outline around each design.
Fully transparent background (PNG with alpha channel), no white background, no fabric, no hoop, no shadow.
```
- Bundle 07: đổi palette thành "pastel pink, white, silver, soft red".
- Bundle 05: thêm "faces only on animals and objects, no faces on holy figures".
