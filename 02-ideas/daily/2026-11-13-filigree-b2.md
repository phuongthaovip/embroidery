## Prompt

**Style chung dùng cho mẫu lẻ:**
> Style: machine embroidery mockup on a plain solid pure white fabric, elegant LINEWORK embroidery only: thin satin outlines and fine running stitch, interior details made of elegant filigree swirls and scrolls, NO large fill areas. Only 3 thread colors as listed, all clearly visible on white fabric. A few small 4-point sparkle stars around. No frame, no border, no title. Spell all words exactly as written. Completely flat white #FFFFFF background, no checkerboard.

**Style chung dùng cho bundle:** giống style mẫu lẻ, nhưng thay câu "No frame, no border, no title" bằng câu sau:
> Each design in its own rounded rectangle frame with a thin outline and the design name in small serif lettering below, laid out as a clean grid like a product bundle sheet, with the bundle title in elegant script at the top.
