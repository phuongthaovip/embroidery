## Prompt

- **Ảnh 1:** "Create a square 1:1 image. The words "Merry Christmas" written in [mô tả font]." + style `FG_S` (style filigree mẫu lẻ: thêu nét, lòng chữ xoắn filigree, không tô kín mảng màu, vải trắng).
- **Ảnh 2, gửi tiếp trong cùng chat:**
  > Now create a square 1:1 image of the complete uppercase alphabet in exactly the same filigree font, decorations and thread colors as the previous image. All 26 letters exactly once each, in 4 centered rows: row 1 "A B C D E F G", row 2 "H I J K L M N", row 3 "O P Q R S T U", row 4 "V W X Y Z". Every letter the same size and style. Elegant LINEWORK only: thin satin outlines, fine running stitch and filigree swirls, NO solid filled letters. Plain solid pure white fabric, flat white #FFFFFF background, no frame, no title, no checkerboard.
