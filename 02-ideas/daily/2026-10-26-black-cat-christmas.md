## 3. Chi tiết + prompt

Đuôi prompt dùng chung cho cả lô (thay đuôi chuẩn, giảm còn 4 màu theo gu đơn giản):
```
Style: machine embroidery design mockup, realistic satin and fill stitch texture,
flat colors only (max 4 thread colors), bold clean outlines, no gradients, no shading,
no fur strands, no tiny details or thin lines, simple shapes that can be digitized,
centered on plain white background, fits a 4x4 inch hoop, square 1:1 image.
```

### 1. Black Cat Santa Hat Minimal
- Keyword: black cat christmas embroidery design, santa hat cat embroidery, minimalist christmas cat
- Màu: đen, đỏ, trắng, vàng (mắt). Cỡ 2–5 in, hợp ngực trái.
- Prompt: `A cute black cat sitting, seen from the front, wearing a red Santa hat with white pom pom, big round yellow eyes, solid black flat body silhouette with no fur detail, small white whiskers.` + đuôi

### 2. Meowy Christmas Black Cat Bundle (8)
- Keyword: meowy christmas embroidery bundle, black cat christmas bundle, christmas cat embroidery designs
- Prompt: `A set of 8 separate small embroidery designs arranged in a neat 3x3 grid with one empty center, each design isolated with white space around it, consistent style, palette black, red, white, green: 1) black cat with Santa hat and the words "Meowy Christmas", 2) black cat peeking out of a red stocking, 3) black cat with a green wreath around its neck, 4) black cat sleeping curled up with a red bow, 5) black cat holding a candy cane, 6) black cat with reindeer antlers, 7) black cat batting a red ornament, 8) black cat face with Santa hat only.` + đuôi
- Lưu ý: AI hay viết sai "Meowy" → kiểm tra chữ trước khi list.

### 3. Stacked Black Cats Christmas Tree
- Keyword: black cat christmas tree embroidery, stacked cats embroidery, funny cat christmas tree
- Prompt: `Six black cats stacked on top of each other forming the shape of a triangle Christmas tree, the top cat wears a yellow star, small red and green round ornaments hanging from their paws, a small brown tree trunk at the bottom, big yellow eyes, flat black bodies.` + đuôi
- Cỡ 4–7 in (to hơn để mắt mèo không bị quá nhỏ).

### 4. Black Cat Bauble Bundle (4)
- Keyword: christmas cat ornament embroidery, black cat bauble embroidery design, striped ornament embroidery
- Prompt: `A set of 4 separate embroidery designs in a 2x2 grid, each a round Christmas bauble ornament with a gold cap: 1) red and white stripes with a black cat in the center, 2) green and white stripes with a black cat wearing a Santa hat, 3) red gingham with a sleeping black cat, 4) white with a black cat face and snowflakes. Same style, palette red, green, black, white, gold.` + đuôi

### 5. ITH Black Cat Ornament Set (6)
- Keyword: ith christmas ornament embroidery, in the hoop black cat ornament, christmas cat ornament ith
- Prompt: `In-the-hoop felt ornament embroidery designs, a set of 6 in a 3x2 grid, each a flat black cat shape with a satin stitch border and a small hanging loop on top: cat with Santa hat, cat with red scarf, cat with green bow, cat sitting in a stocking, cat with antlers, cat with a gift box. Visible felt fabric, palette black, red, green, white.` + đuôi
- Cần designer xác nhận: ITH 2 mặt hay 1 mặt.

### 6. Black Cat Tangled in Christmas Lights
- Keyword: funny christmas cat embroidery, black cat christmas lights embroidery, meowy and bright
- Prompt: `A grumpy black cat tangled in a string of colorful Christmas lights (red, green, yellow bulbs), annoyed expression with half-closed yellow eyes, the words "Meowy & Bright" in bold rounded letters below.` + đuôi
- Em dùng câu "Meowy & Bright" thay vì "It's fine I'm fine" (câu meme nhiều shop đã dùng), để mẫu khác biệt hơn.

### 7. Black Cat Hugging Candy Cane
- Keyword: candy cane cat embroidery, black cat christmas embroidery, cute christmas kitten design
- Prompt: `A cute chubby black cat hugging a big red and white striped candy cane, wearing a tiny red Santa hat, big yellow eyes, flat black body.` + đuôi

### 8. Black Cat Mama Christmas
- Keyword: black cat mom embroidery, cat mama christmas embroidery design, cat lover christmas gift
- Prompt: `The words "Black Cat Mama" in bold red retro rounded letters, a small black cat with a Santa hat sitting on top of the letters, two small green holly leaves.` + đuôi
- Có thể thêm biến thể "Black Cat Dad" để thành bundle 2.

### 9. Christmas Black Cat Mini Icons (6)
- Keyword: mini christmas embroidery designs, black cat mini embroidery, tiny cat christmas embroidery bundle
- Prompt: `A set of 6 tiny simple embroidery icons in a 3x2 grid, each isolated with white space: 1) black cat in a red gift box with a bow, 2) black cat in a red stocking, 3) black cat in a white mug, 4) black cat inside a green wreath, 5) black cat face with Santa hat, 6) black cat paw print with a holly leaf. Max 3 colors per icon, very simple shapes for 1.5 inch size.` + đuôi
- Cỡ 1–2.5 in, cho cổ tay áo, mũ, vớ.

### 10. Black Cat Red Scarf Snowflakes
- Keyword: winter cat embroidery design, black cat snowflake embroidery, christmas cat scarf embroidery
- Prompt: `A black cat sitting, wearing a long red knitted scarf (flat color, simple stripe pattern), looking up at four big simple white snowflakes, big yellow eyes.` + đuôi
- Lưu ý: bông tuyết phải to, nét dày ≥ 1 mm, không làm bông tuyết li ti.
